# Adomeh

**Open, community-made English translations of the classical physicians, read against the body.**

Adomeh reads the physicians who built the medicine of fifteen centuries beside the anatomy they describe. The translations are openly licensed and edited in the open: anyone can propose a correction, and every accepted one makes the edition better for everyone who reads it. The atlas that sets them beside a 3D human body is at **[adomeh.com](https://adomeh.com)**.

| Physician | What is here | Translated from |
|---|---|---|
| **Hippocrates** (c. 460–370 BCE) | 17 works in Francis Adams's English (1849), checked against the printed volumes | Greek |
| **Galen** (129–c. 216) | 96 works: 95 in Adomeh's English, each read twice against the Greek, and *On the Natural Faculties* in Brock's English | Greek |
| **Avicenna** (980–1037) | *The Canon of Medicine*, all five books, in Adomeh's English | Arabic |

The translations began as machine translation from the original texts and were then read against them passage by passage. They are a reading aid, not a critical edition, and they will get better with your help.

### Help make them better

Spotted a mistranslation, a dropped negation or a term used inconsistently? Corrections are welcome from anyone who reads Greek or Arabic, and from anyone who notices that a sentence does not make sense. Open an issue with the [correction form](https://github.com/Adomeh/texts/issues/new?template=correction.yml), or edit the text yourself in **[Adomeh/texts](https://github.com/Adomeh/texts)**, where every work sits beside its Greek or Arabic original. The [contributor guide](https://github.com/Adomeh/texts/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) is short.
